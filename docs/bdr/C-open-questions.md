# C. Open questions and standing assumptions

Items marked Closed were answered by the customer and are retained so the history is visible. Everything else is still carried as a team assumption. Priority reflects how many stories the answer would touch. O-20 to O-44 are the ambiguities in the Week 7 Customer Changes (C-65 to C-70); each records the default the stories are written to and the exact question to put to the customer at the next Q&A (T-73). An acceptance criterion that depends on one is tagged "(assumes O-xx)" in the backlog.
