The team has chosen to finish every remaining Release 1 story without planning a
Sprint 5. A read-only inventory found 30 unfinished Release 1 stories: 18 are
in Sprint 4 (64 points), and 12 remain outside Sprint 4 (3 estimated at 11
points and 9 unestimated). This is the scope target, not yet a capacity plan for
all 30 stories.

remains available as history and reflects the earlier scope proposal. This
repository note records the latest scope gap visible in Jira so the team can
replan it without implying the unallocated work is already committed to Sprint
4. It does not change story requirements, estimates, statuses or sprint fields.
